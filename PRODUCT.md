# Product brief

Film Lab helps a coach move from an idea to a teachable 7-on-7 play: choose an alignment, place players, draw timed routes, name the intent, and replay the result. The app is intentionally local-first so a practice plan remains available without accounts or connectivity.

The v1 boundary is deliberate: 7v7 modeling, editor, Film Room playback, starter library, playbook organization, and JSON sharing. Backend services, accounts, sync, NFL content, and native mobile are outside the product.
